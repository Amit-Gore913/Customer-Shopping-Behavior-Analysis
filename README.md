Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

1) About the Project

I worked on this project to analyze customer shopping data and understand how customers purchase products, spend money, use discounts, choose shipping options, and interact with subscriptions.

I used Python for data cleaning, MySQL for SQL analysis, and Power BI to create an interactive dashboard. I also created a presentation to summarize the main business insights and recommendations.

2) What I Worked On

- Cleaned and prepared the customer shopping dataset using Python and Pandas
- Handled missing review ratings and prepared the data for analysis
- Created customer age groups and converted purchase frequency into days
- Loaded the cleaned data into MySQL
- Wrote SQL queries to answer different business questions
- Created a Power BI dashboard to present the analysis
- Created a presentation summarizing the findings and possible business actions

  Analysis Done

3) Some of the questions I explored were:

- Which gender generates more revenue?
- Which products have the highest average ratings?
- How do Standard and Express shipping compare?
- How does spending differ between subscribed and non-subscribed customers?
- Which products have more discounts?
- How can customers be divided into New, Returning, and Loyal customers?
- Which products are popular within each category?
- How much revenue comes from different age groups?
- How strong is subscription penetration?
- How many repeat buyers are subscribed?
- Which products appear to depend more on discounts?
- Which products have strong ratings and which products have high order volume?

Key Insights

4) From the analysis and presentation, some of the main findings were:

- The dataset contains 3,900 purchases across 4 categories, 25 products, and 50 locations.
- Most customers fall into the Loyal segment, with 3,116 Loyal customers compared with 701 Returning and 83 New customers.
- Male customers generated higher revenue than female customers in the analysis.
- Clothing was the highest-revenue category, followed by Accessories.
- Young Adults generated the highest revenue among the age groups.
- Only 27% of customers were subscribed, while 73% were not subscribed.
- Average purchase amounts were very similar between subscribed and non-subscribed customers.
- A large number of repeat buyers were not subscribed, showing an opportunity to improve subscription conversion.
- Hat, Sneakers, Coat, Sweater, and Pants had some of the highest discount rates.
- Gloves, Sandals, Boots, Hat, and Skirt were among the highest-rated products.
- Some products had strong order volume even when they were not the highest-rated products, showing that product popularity and ratings are not always the same.
- Express shipping showed a slightly higher average purchase value than Standard shipping in the presentation.

5) Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, I identified three main areas for improvement:

1. **Target repeat buyers**
   - Offer subscription benefits when customers reach a strong repeat-purchase point, such as their fifth purchase.

2. **Review discount-heavy products**
   - Check products with high discount rates and test more focused promotions to help protect margins.

3. **Use both ratings and sales volume**
   - Use highly rated products to build customer confidence while also using popular products to guide merchandising and campaigns.
